其他常用介词

初级语法 · 代冠名天平介词 第 7 节 · 对应讲义 P13–P15 · 共 — 个判分点

介词其他常用介词 扫码看讲解

这些介词没有统一规律，靠例句记最快 —— 先把下面 15 句读一遍，再做题。

讲义原例句（15 句）
  • in about 40 days　We will say goodbye to our junior high in about 40 days.　—— in + 一段时间 = 多久之后
  • for 10 years　Tumei has lived in Chongqing for 10 years.　—— for + 时间段 = 持续了多久
  • except Jack　The whole class went camping except Jack.　—— 除了……之外（不含）
  • without your help　I can't finish this project without your help. I am not able to do this by myself.
  • by the river　Jenny is walking by the river.　—— by = 在……旁边
  • by practicing　She learned to ride a bike by practicing every day.　—— by + doing = 通过做某事
  • Through hard work　Through hard work, she was able to achieve her long-awaited promotion.
  • kind of Lisa　It is very kind of Lisa to help me with my Maths.　—— of + 人，评价这个人的品质
  • at a lower price　We usually buy some things online on November 11th every year at a lower price.
  • for PE class　I need a pair of sports shoes for PE class.　—— for = 为了
  • for supper　What did you eat for supper?　—— for + 三餐 = 作为某一餐
  • good for your health　Vegetables are good for your health.　—— 对……有益
  • along the street　Go along the street until you reach the end.　—— 沿着
  • between A and B　I couldn't choose between the blue shirt and the red shirt.　—— 两者之间
  • among the students　The teacher distributed the cookies among the students.　—— 三者以上之间
最常考的四组对比：
· for + 时间段　←→　since + 时间点
· between 两者之间　←→　among 三者以上之间
· except 除了……（不含）　←→　besides 除了……（还含）
· with 带有某特征（with glasses）　←→　in 穿着某衣服／颜色（in red）

三个 good 一起记：be good for 有益　be good at 擅长　be good to 对人好
